Optional Text Message Notifications

Appointment confirmation and reminder text messages are available for your Tithing Declaration appointment.

Yes, I agree to receive SMS appointment confirmation and reminder messages from the Tithing Declaration Scheduler.

Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. SMS consent is optional and is not required to schedule an appointment.

How consent is obtained

The SMS checkbox is displayed directly on the first page of the scheduling form. It is unchecked by default. An individual must affirmatively check the box to opt in. The individual may schedule an appointment without consenting to SMS messages.
