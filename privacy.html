Privacy Policy

Tithing Declaration Scheduler

Information We Collect

When you schedule an appointment, we may collect your first and last name, phone number, email address, selected appointment date and time, and your choice regarding SMS text message notifications.

How We Use Information

We use this information to schedule and manage appointments and to provide appointment confirmations, reminders, and related communications.

SMS Text Messaging

SMS messaging is optional. Text messages are sent only to individuals who affirmatively opt in by checking the SMS consent checkbox. Messages are limited to appointment confirmations and reminders. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

We do not share, sell, or provide your mobile phone number or messaging consent data to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Information Sharing

Your personal information is not sold or used for advertising or marketing. Service providers may process information only as necessary to provide the scheduling and messaging services.

Opting Out

You may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP. Reply HELP for help.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be directed to the administrator of the Tithing Declaration Scheduler.
