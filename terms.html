Tithing Declaration Scheduler

SMS Messaging Program

Individuals who voluntarily opt in to SMS messaging through the Tithing Declaration Scheduler may receive text messages related to their scheduled Tithing Declaration appointment. Messages may include an appointment confirmation and an appointment reminder.

Consent

SMS messaging is optional. Consent to receive text messages is provided by affirmatively selecting the SMS consent checkbox on the appointment scheduling form. Consent to receive SMS messages is not required to schedule an appointment.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on appointment activity. Typically, an opted-in individual will receive an appointment confirmation and may receive an appointment reminder related to the scheduled appointment.

Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply depending on the individual's mobile carrier and service plan.

Carrier Disclaimer

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Opt-Out

Reply STOP to opt out of SMS messages at any time. After opting out, no further SMS messages will be sent unless the individual subsequently provides consent again.

Help

Reply HELP for assistance with SMS messaging. For additional assistance, contact the administrator of the Tithing Declaration Scheduler.

Privacy

Personal information and SMS consent information are handled in accordance with the Tithing Declaration Scheduler Privacy Policy. We do not sell or share SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Contact

Questions regarding these Terms & Conditions may be directed to the administrator of the Tithing Declaration Scheduler.
